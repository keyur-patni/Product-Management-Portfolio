# Prioritization 🔥

Prioritization helps the clinic focus on the features that deliver the highest impact early on. For this product, the goal is simple: reduce wait times, improve coordination, and create a predictable patient experience as quickly as possible.

To do that, we use a combination of RICE, MoSCoW, and Value‑Effort scoring. Each method highlights different aspects of value, and together they create a balanced prioritization approach.

## RICE Scoring (Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort)

RICE helps identify features that deliver the most value relative to the effort required.

- Digital check‑in — High reach, high impact, low effort

- Real‑time patient status tracking — High impact, medium effort

- Triage queue & rooming alerts — Medium reach, high impact

- Provider ready‑to‑see dashboard — High impact, medium effort

- Wait‑time notifications — High reach, medium effort

- Operational analytics — Medium reach, high long‑term value

- Billing summary — Medium reach, medium impact

- Role‑based access control — High confidence, low effort

These RICE scores help identify the MVP foundation: check‑in, status tracking, triage, provider workflow, and notifications.

## MoSCoW Prioritization (Must / Should / Could / Won’t)

MoSCoW helps categorize features based on urgency and necessity.

### Must‑Have

- Digital check‑in

- Patient status tracking

- Triage queue

- Provider dashboard

- Wait‑time notifications

These are essential for reducing wait times and improving flow.

### Should‑Have

- Lab order tracking

- Billing summary

- Follow‑up scheduling

- These improve the end‑to‑end experience but aren’t required for MVP.

### Could‑Have

- Predictive wait‑time modeling

- Peak‑hour analytics

- Custom triage rules

These add value but can be delivered later.

### Won’t‑Have (for now)

- Advanced AI triage scoring

- Full EHR integration

These are complex and not required for the initial release.

## Value‑Effort Matrix

This method helps visualize quick wins vs. long‑term investments.

### High Value / Low Effort (Quick Wins)

- Digital check‑in

- Role‑based access

- Wait‑time notifications

### High Value / High Effort (Strategic Investments)

- Patient status tracking

- Provider dashboard

- Triage queue

### Medium Value / Low Effort

- Billing summary

- Follow‑up scheduling

### Medium Value / High Effort

- Operational analytics

- Lab coordination

## How This Drives the MVP

- Based on all three prioritization methods, the MVP focuses on:

- Digital check‑in

- Patient status tracking

- Triage queue & rooming

- Provider dashboard

- Wait‑time notifications

These features deliver the fastest improvement in wait times, staff coordination, and patient satisfaction.

Everything else builds on this foundation.
