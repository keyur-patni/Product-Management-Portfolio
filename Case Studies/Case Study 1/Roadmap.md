# Roadmap 🗺️

The roadmap focuses on delivering the highest‑impact improvements first: reducing wait times, improving staff coordination, and creating a predictable patient experience. It’s structured into four phases that build toward a fully integrated patient flow system.

## Phase 1 - Foundation (MVP)

This phase delivers the core features needed to make patient flow visible and predictable.

- Digital check‑in

- Patient status tracking

- Triage queue & rooming

- Provider ready‑to‑see dashboard

- Wait‑time notifications

Outcome:  
Clinics gain real‑time visibility into patient movement, reducing delays and confusion for both patients and staff.

## Phase 2 — Workflow Expansion

This phase strengthens coordination across clinical roles and expands the end‑to‑end visit experience.

- Digital intake forms

- Room availability alerts

- Provider visit status updates

- Follow‑up scheduling

- Billing summary & digital checkout

Outcome:  
Staff handoffs become smoother, patients experience fewer repeated steps, and checkout becomes faster and more transparent.

## Phase 3 — Clinical Coordination & Labs

This phase connects providers and lab technicians to reduce turnaround time and improve continuity of care.

- Lab order creation

- Lab status tracking

- Result notifications

- Integration with follow‑up workflows

Outcome:  
Providers receive lab results faster, patients move through visits more efficiently, and clinics reduce idle time between steps.

## Phase 4 — Analytics & Administration

This phase adds intelligence, configurability, and operational insights.

- Operational analytics dashboard

- Predictive wait‑time modeling

- Peak‑hour analysis

- Role‑based access control

- Custom triage rules

- Form builder for intake questions

Outcome:  
Clinic managers gain actionable insights, staff workflows become customizable, and the system adapts to each clinic’s operational needs.
