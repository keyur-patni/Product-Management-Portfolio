# Overview 🏥

The Patient Flow Management System is a digital platform designed to help healthcare clinics manage how patients move through their visit, from scheduling to check‑in, triage, consultation, lab work, billing, and follow‑up. Clinics often rely on manual processes, fragmented communication, and outdated tools, which leads to long wait times, bottlenecks, and frustration for both patients and staff.

This product brings the entire patient journey into one coordinated, real‑time experience. It gives clinics visibility into where each patient is, what they need next, and how staff can work together more efficiently. For patients, it creates a smoother, more predictable visit. For clinic teams, it reduces operational friction and improves throughput.

The goal is simple, help clinics deliver care that feels organized, timely, and patient‑centered without adding extra burden to already busy staff.
