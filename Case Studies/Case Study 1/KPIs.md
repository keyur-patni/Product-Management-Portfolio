# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) 📊

Improving patient flow isn’t just about making visits feel smoother — it’s about creating measurable operational impact. These KPIs help the clinic understand whether the Patient Flow Management System is reducing delays, improving coordination, and enhancing the overall patient experience.

## Operational Efficiency

- Average patient wait time from check‑in to triage

- Average wait time from triage to provider consultation

- Total visit duration (end‑to‑end throughput)

- Room utilization rate

- Number of patients seen per provider per day

## Staff Productivity

- Time spent on manual intake tasks

- Number of back‑and‑forth status checks between staff

- Triage completion time

- Provider idle time between visits

## Patient Experience

- Patient satisfaction score (post‑visit survey)

- Percentage of patients receiving real‑time updates

- Number of repeated questions or duplicate form entries

- No‑show rate and late‑arrival rate

## Workflow Reliability

- Percentage of patients with complete intake data before provider visit

- Number of delayed handoffs (front desk → nurse → provider)

- Frequency of bottlenecks during peak hours

- Accuracy of predicted wait times

## Business Impact

- Daily patient throughput

- Revenue per clinic day

- Cost savings from reduced manual processes

- Staff overtime hours
