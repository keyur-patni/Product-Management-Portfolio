# User Personas 👥

1. Patient
   Patients come to the clinic for scheduled appointments, urgent visits, follow‑ups, or lab work. Their biggest frustrations are long wait times, unclear expectations, repeated questions, and not knowing where they are in the process. They want a visit that feels organized, predictable, and respectful of their time.

**Needs**

- A smooth check‑in experience

- Clear communication about wait times

- Guidance on what happens next

- Reduced paperwork and repeated questions

- A sense of transparency and control

**Pain Points**

- Long, unpredictable waits

- Confusing or repetitive intake steps

- Lack of updates during the visit

2. Front Desk Staff
   Front desk teams handle scheduling, check‑ins, forms, insurance verification, and patient intake. They often juggle multiple tasks at once and are the first to feel the pressure when the clinic gets busy.

**Needs**

- A simple, fast check‑in workflow

- Real‑time visibility into patient status

- Fewer manual steps and repeated data entry

- Clear communication with nurses and providers

**Pain Points**

- Overwhelming peak‑hour traffic

- Manual triage and room assignment

- Constant interruptions from staff asking for patient status

3. Nurse / Medical Assistant
   Nurses and MAs manage triage, vitals, rooming, handoffs, and coordination between providers. They rely heavily on timing and communication to keep the clinic moving.

**Needs**

- A clear view of which patients are ready for triage

- Smooth handoffs between front desk and providers

- Alerts when rooms open or patients are ready

- Reduced back‑and‑forth communication

**Pain Points**

- Bottlenecks during triage

- Unclear patient readiness

- Inefficient room utilization

4. Provider (Doctor / PA / NP)
   Providers need to move efficiently between patients while maintaining quality of care. Delays in triage, rooming, or lab results directly impact their productivity.

**Needs**

- Predictable patient flow

- Visibility into who is ready and who is waiting

- Fewer delays between visits

- Clear communication from staff

**Pain Points**

- Idle time between patients

- Missing or incomplete intake information

- Unpredictable visit durations

5. Clinic Manager
   Clinic managers oversee operations, staffing, throughput, and patient satisfaction. They need reliable data to understand bottlenecks and make informed decisions.

**Needs**

- Real‑time operational dashboards

- Insights into wait times, throughput, and bottlenecks

- Tools to improve staffing and scheduling

- A consistent patient experience

**Pain Points**

- Limited visibility into daily operations

- Difficulty identifying root causes of delays

- Inconsistent patient satisfaction scores
