# Epics (Template + Examples)

Epics represent large bodies of work that deliver significant value and are broken down into smaller user stories. This document provides reusable templates and examples applicable across any product or domain.

🧩 1. Epic Definition

An Epic is a large, high‑level initiative that:

- Delivers meaningful value

- Spans multiple sprints

- Requires multiple user stories

- Can be broken down into smaller, testable units

- Aligns with product strategy and roadmap themes

Epics help teams organize complex work into manageable, trackable components.

🧩 3. Example Epics (Generic Portfolio Examples)

## Epic 1 — Digital Check‑In Experience

Goal: Reduce front‑desk congestion and improve patient onboarding.
Problem: Patients wait too long during peak hours due to manual check‑in.
KPIs:

- Reduce check‑in time by 40%

- Increase digital adoption to 70%

- Reduce front‑desk workload by 30%

## User Stories:

- Digital Check‑In Story

- Patient identity validation

- Error handling for invalid inputs

- Confirmation screen

## Epic 2 — Real‑Time Patient Status Tracking

Goal: Improve transparency and reduce patient anxiety during visits.
Problem: Patients often don’t know what stage of the visit they’re in.
KPIs:

- Increase patient satisfaction scores

- Reduce “Where am I in the process?” questions by 50%

## User Stories:

- Status timeline display

- Automatic status updates

- Timestamp tracking

- Notification triggers

## Epic 3 — Triage Workflow Optimization

Goal: Improve triage efficiency and reduce wait times.
Problem: Nurses lack a centralized dashboard to prioritize patients.
KPIs:

- Reduce triage wait time by 25%

- Improve prioritization accuracy

## User Stories:

- Priority scoring

- Triage dashboard

- Patient sorting logic

- Status updates

## Epic 4 — Provider Tools & Visit Management

Goal: Give providers a unified view of patient information.
Problem: Providers switch between multiple systems during visits.
KPIs:

- Reduce provider screen switching by 60%

- Improve visit throughput

## User Stories:

- Provider dashboard

- Lab results view

- Visit notes

- Prescription workflow

## Epic 5 — Follow‑Up Scheduling & Care Continuity

Goal: Ensure patients complete their care plan.
Problem: Follow‑up appointments are often missed or forgotten.
KPIs:

- Increase follow‑up scheduling rate

- Reduce no‑shows

## User Stories:

- Calendar selection

- Time slot selection

- Appointment confirmation

- Email notification

🧩 5. Best Practices for Writing Epics

- Focus on value, not features

- Keep epics large, but not vague

- Ensure epics can be completed within 1–3 months

- Break epics into independent user stories

- Align epics with roadmap themes

- Include KPIs to measure success

🧩 6. Epic Anti‑Patterns (Avoid These)

- Epics that are too small (should be a story instead)

- Epics that describe UI instead of value

- Epics without measurable outcomes

- Epics that depend on one giant story

- Epics that mix unrelated goals
